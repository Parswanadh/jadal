Jadal.

Canal water shared by volume, not by hours.

IEEE-CIS hackathon · LLMs + Agentic AI

Warabandi shares hours, not water.

Each farmer gets fixed hours on a weekly roster.

Illustrative: eight outlets, equal one-hour turns.

Equal hours. Unequal water.

  • Upstream turns overrun and cut the flow below.
  • Seepage and lag leave tail-end flow weak and late.
  • Schedule changes miss farmers outside the chat group.

Jadal plans by volume, then turns it into time.

FAO-56 is the UN's standard method for estimating crop water need. The amber step is the human gate: nothing is allocated without the coordinator.
