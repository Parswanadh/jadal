## If asked

**Why do you not claim that the tail meets 42 percent of its need?**

> The original handoff quoted it, but core does not reproduce it. On the seed, core's planned need-met is 280.8 percent at the head and 205.5 at the tail, which is over-allocation. The 42 came from an illustrative mock formula in the web app. We checked, so we do not claim it.

**Do SMS and WhatsApp work?**

> No. The API accepts those channels, records a queued contact and answers that it is simulated. No transport exists. The interface now says so. Twilio also blocks SMS to India on a trial account.

**Is the Telugu native-reviewed?**

> No. It is machine-written. We prepared a review pack of 680 strings, with automatic flags, for a native speaker to work through. Until then we describe it as unreviewed.

**Do live phone calls work?**

> In testing, real Twilio calls connected and were answered. But the account is a trial with a zero balance, and the quota cut out once mid-session. So we do not call live calls demo-reliable. That is a funding step, not a code change.

**Are the flows measured at the canal?**

> No. They come from the seed scenario's decay model, with Q0 of 0.15 cubic metres per second and a decay constant of 0.00012 per metre. They are modelled. Field gauge data would be the next step.

**Who is allowed to approve? Is there authentication?**

> Not on the server. The role gate is client-side by design in this build, and the approve route records a fixed coordinator identity. Approval is a required workflow step, but who may perform it is not yet enforced. Server-side identity, roles and per-user audit are the first hardening item.

**Can anyone reset the demo or change the clock?**

> The reset and advance routes exist and have no authentication in this build. They are demo controls and must be removed or locked down before anything real runs. We do not reset or advance during a call.

**Is the event log tamper-proof?**

> No. It is append-only by convention: nothing in the code updates or deletes a row, but there are no database triggers and no hash chain. Making it tamper-evident is a known next step.

**How are farmers' phone numbers protected?**

> They are stored in plain text in the database, and the outbound limit is per handset. There is no masking or encryption of stored numbers yet. That needs doing before real farmers are loaded.

**Does it scale to many canals?**

> We have not measured scale. The schema keeps a canal table so more canals are new rows, not a schema change, but we have run one demo canal. We would not quote a number.
