Canal water shared by volume, not by hours.

On unlined canals, seepage robs tail-end farms of up to 29.4% of their flow per second. Traditional schedules divide hours equally, leaving the tail starved.

Jadal pairs open-channel hydraulics with FAO-56 crop physics to compute equal-volume turns. With Telugu voice outreach for non-smartphone farmers and double-entry conservation ledger verification, Jadal brings fair water to the tail end.
