Canal water shared by volume, not by hours.

For generations, canal irrigation in South Asia has relied on warabandi — a rigid schedule of equal hours per farmer. Because unlined canals lose up to 29.4% of flow to soil seepage and travel lag, tail-end farmers receive significantly less water than upstream farms. Jadal replaces hour allocation with physics-computed volume delivery under a transparent double-entry ledger.

Root Cause Analysis

Warabandi shares hours, not water.

Under traditional rules, every farmer receives a weekly time window proportional to acreage. The paper register records the roster as "equal and fair." In physical reality, three severe failures destroy tail crops:

1. Hours ≠ Water

Water is lost every metre through unlined canal banks. A one-hour turn at Outlet 1 (300 m) delivers 521 m³, while the same one-hour turn at Outlet 8 (2,900 m) delivers only 381 m³. Tail farmers lose nearly a third of their water before it reaches the field gate.

2. Invisible Agronomic Need

Fixed hour rosters cannot adapt to crop stages. Rice in flowering requires uninterrupted ponding; harvesting cotton requires zero water; sandy soils deplete twice as fast as clay. The old register has no mechanism to absorb crop biology or rain events.

3. Unacknowledged Changes

Schedule updates sent over WhatsApp chat groups fail farmers without smartphones. Night releases (18:00–06:00) arrive unwarned, causing uncollected water to flood into ditches while crops dry up.

Deterministic Hydraulics

Equal Volume: Pricing time with local discharge.

Jadal preserves the warabandi rotation structure but prices each turn's time from its required volume and physical conveyance losses. The core formula implemented in @jadal/core/hydraulics is:

Tᵢ = Vᵢ / Q(xᵢ)

Where Tᵢ is turn duration in seconds, Vᵢ is approved crop water requirement in m³, and Q(xᵢ) = Q₀ · exp(-k · xᵢ) is the flow rate reaching outlet xᵢ after exponential seepage loss along chainage xᵢ.

System Governance & Invariants

LLMs propose · Core computes · Coordinator approves.

AI in public resource management must have inviolable boundaries. Jadal strictly enforces separation between natural-language understanding, mathematical execution, and human authority:

Tier 01
LLMs & Agents Propose

System-1 fast classifiers transcribe Telugu speech, score urgency, and detect farmer intents. System-2 reasoning agents draft recommendations. Agents never perform arithmetic or modify databases directly.

Tier 02
Deterministic Core Computes

The TypeScript core package (@jadal/core) computes all numbers: FAO-56 crop water demand, Manning open-channel velocities, seepage lags, and double-entry ledger transfers. Tested by 85 unit tests.

Tier 03
Coordinator Approves

Every roster, emergency quota grant, and schedule adjustment remains in "proposed" status until an authorized Water User Association (WUA) coordinator explicitly reviews and signs off on the dashboard.

Agronomic Science

Crop water demand rooted in FAO-56 (2025).

Rather than rough rule-of-thumb quotas, Jadal implements the United Nations FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper No. 56 standard. Entitlements are calculated daily from reference evapotranspiration ($\text{ET}_0$), crop stage growth coefficients ($K_c$), and soil infiltration rates.

Crop Evapotranspiration & Soil Infiltration

$$\text{ET}_c = K_c \cdot \text{ET}_0$$ Net field requirement accounts for soil water retention, daily rainfall deductions, and deep percolation in heavy Vertisols (clay loam) versus sandy loam soils.

Accessible to every farmer, smartphone or not.

Night Release Protocol & Acknowledgment

Canal delivery cannot change unilaterally:

  • • No Smartphone Parity: Outbound voice calls automatically placed to f5 and f8; DTMF key 1 or Telugu speech «సరే» registers legal acknowledgment.
  • • Night Warning Calls: When Release Window 2 opens at 19:00 IST after sunset, a mandatory warning call fires at 18:00 IST (1 hour prior) to alert farmers before gates lift.
  • • Valid Only Upon Acknowledgment: Turn shifts do not take effect until affected downstream farmers acknowledge.
Cryptographic & Accounting Integrity

Double-entry ledger: Conserving every cubic metre.

In traditional irrigation, water theft and political favouritism are hidden by vague time registers. Jadal records every drop in a strict double-entry ledger where conservation is an unbreakable mathematical invariant.

Supply (180,000 m³) = ∑ Quotas + Buffer + ∑ Delivered + Losses

Tested and verified by apps/api/src/e2e.test.ts#L262: conservation_ok: true.

Overruns Are Borrowed

When farmer f1 receives 40 m³ urgent water today, his seasonal quota account immediately credits -40 m³. Overruns stop being free theft.

Early Harvest Buffer Pool

When cotton farmer f3 completes harvesting early, his remaining quota moves to a transparent public buffer rather than being hoarded or sold.

Bilingual Public Audit

An autonomous Auditor Agent compiles Telugu and English audit memos so Water User Association meetings examine clear balances instead of shouting.
