Everyone got their hours. The tail still lost the crop. Hours are not water.

Introducing Jadal: open-channel hydraulics and equal-volume turn distribution for gravity irrigation canals.

- Pure Manning conveyance physics: Outlet 1 delivers 0.145 m³/s (3.5% loss); Outlet 8 delivers 0.106 m³/s (29.4% loss).
- Equal volume allocation: turn durations scale with local flow (Tᵢ = Vᵢ / Q(xᵢ)) so tail turns run longer to deliver fair water.
- Strict human gating: LLMs propose, deterministic core computes, coordinator approves. Nothing reaches a farmer without approval.
- Spoken Telugu telephony outreach with rate-limited dispatch and mathematical double-entry conservation ledger (Debits = Credits).

*Note: Telugu voice audio and captions are machine-written and pending native speaker review (docs/review/telugu-native-review.md).

Built with TypeScript, Cloudflare Workers, and open-channel hydraulics.
